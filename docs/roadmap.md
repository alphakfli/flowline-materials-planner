# Roadmap

Ideas for later versions, roughly in priority order.

1. **Allocation optimizer**: re-sequence projects and re-peg supply to maximize on-time projects (today allocation
   follows build-start order and priority).
2. **Substitutes and borrowing**: allow alternate parts and show which later project a part could be borrowed from.
3. **In-house assembly capacity**: check build slots per line or bay, not just material.
4. **Forecast waterfall for CMs**: keep every forecast version sent and show how stable it was.
5. **Supplier confirmations**: track promised-date changes per PO line (confirmation reliability).
6. **E&O reserve**: ageing buckets and reserve policy for excess and obsolete stock.
7. **Direct SAP connector**: read the tables in [sap-mapping.md](sap-mapping.md) through OData or an RFC extract.
8. **Week buckets** and a factory calendar.
